### Description

```text
Halden is a Framer template for photographers who publish work as series, not as a dump of single frames.

Paper and ink. Syne for titles, IBM Plex for the catalog. Home is a drifting plane of stills. Each series opens as a lookbook. Info and Contact live in the nav overlay. Three routes, twelve CMS series, original photography included.

Pages
• Home, Work lookbook, 404
• Overlay: studio note, series index, enquiry form

CMS
• Cover, Year, credits, Type, tags, Body, Featured
• Lookbook is Cover plus Still 1 to 4, each with a caption
• Featured on puts a series on the Home plane. Featured off keeps the detail page.

Keep the pasteboard collection list named "Drift source — do not delete". The Home plane reads from it.
```
