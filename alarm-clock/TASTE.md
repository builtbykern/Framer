## Locked skin — Alarm (despertador-objeto)

The component must read as a **named object**: a small desk alarm / wake clock whose face **is** the countdown.

Visual contract:
1. **Object silhouette** readable at Marketplace thumbnail size (~320px): body + face + optional feet/bells/handle — simplified, not a photo of a real Casio.
2. **Face** carries D/H/M/S (or H/M/S) with clear hierarchy; labels quiet under digits.
3. **Material:** paper/ink or soft metal + glass face — Made-in-May light discipline, not plastic toy.
4. **Motion:** digit change is the only performance (tick / flip / crossfade). Body stays still or has one micro idle (subtle second hand / pulse) that reduces under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Not** a naked flip-clock row floating in void without object framing (that = Marketplace default FAIL).

Parked skins (v1.1+ only): Eclipse dial · Board LED · Cubic digits.
