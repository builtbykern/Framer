# Sill craft

Free one-screen Framer template. Calling card. Not Halden, not Arbour, not a link tree.

## Tokens

- Paper: off-white, near Bruce (`#F4F3F0` or site background). Not Arbour parchment.
- Ink: black type. No second brand color on chrome. Color lives in the still.
- Type: one grotesk. Short name small, all caps, light. Line: sentence case, heavier. List: same family, numbers 01–06.
- Space: Boer air. Large empty field. Do not fill the right column.

## Layout

Desktop: one viewport, no scroll, overflow hidden. Split ~50/50.
Left: name, one line, numbered CMS links (5–6).
Right: 1 still. 2 stacked only if both fit without scroll. No About. No second page.

Mobile: stack type then stills. Slight scroll only if the links do not fit.

## Motion

Hover on a row: number or underline. No GSAP show. No overlay. No cursor follower.
